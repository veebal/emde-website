Exchange rate regimes

Which major currencies does each emerging market currency track, when did that change, and is it changing now? For every country on the map the basket weights on the US dollar, the yen, the euro and sterling are estimated week by week, the dates at which the basket changed are dated with hindsight, and an online filter gives the current probability that a new regime has begun, and a second detector watches the width of the band. Hover over a country for its status; click to open its page. The methodology page describes the procedures, and the subscribe page offers two feeds: new alerts, and retrospective revisions.
