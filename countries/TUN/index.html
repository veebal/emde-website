Tunisia

Data ended The published series for this currency ends on 2019-01-18; the results below stop there.

The basket weights of the TND are estimated on weekly returns measured against the NZD, with the US dollar, the yen, the euro and sterling as the basket. Two procedures run on the same data: a retrospective dating that places the breaks between regimes with the whole sample in hand, and an online filter that, each week, gives the probability that a new regime has begun and watches the residual variance for a change in the width of the band. The methodology page sets out both. Sample: 2010-06-25 to 2019-01-18.

Probability of a new regime

Each week the filter updates the posterior distribution of the time since the last change in the basket weights or the residual variance, using only the data up to that week. The line is the probability that the current regime is younger than 26 weeks. An alert (marker) fires at the first week of a spell of 2 consecutive weeks with this probability at or above 0.50, at most once every 26 weeks. The dashed lines are the breaks dated with the whole sample, for comparison; the filter does not use them.

Change in the variance

A second detector watches the width of the band. Each week the squared one-step residual, standardised by the filter’s own coefficients, is compared with the median squared residual of the preceding 52 weeks (divided by 0.4549 so that it estimates the variance). Two CUSUM statistics accumulate the evidence for a fall of the variance to a third of its reference value and for a rise to 3 times it. An alert (marker) fires when either crosses the horizontal line at 6.25, the logarithm of 520, after which both statistics restart; at most one alert every 26 weeks. A variance alert claims that the change lies within the previous 26 weeks.
